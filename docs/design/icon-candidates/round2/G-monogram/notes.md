Idea: a G monogram where a drawn Bezier stroke (node square + handle) turns into a right-angle machine path ending in a cutter-point ring.
Palette: #1B2630/#2B3B49 charcoal, #F5EBDD cream, #F28C28 orange, #4FB5A5 teal (v2 also #E47A1C/#1F2A33).
v1: dark tile, cream curve discretised into a G-code staircase that forms the G's bar; orange node and cutter ring, teal handle.
v2: orange tile, dark near-full circle G with a sharp 90-degree bar turning down to a cream cutter-point; cream node and handle.
Both read as a G at 16 px; node/handle detail is a bonus at larger sizes.
