# Laser

Choose a laser machine and a laser post in **Setup > Tools, Machines and Materials...**; laser jobs use
power instead of spindle speed.

- Laser cut and fill follow vectors, with optional fill patterns.
- Laser picture burns a bitmap as a raster; laser sketch draws outlines.
- The Preview panel simulates the burn so you can judge power and speed.

To adapt your own post for a laser, read the [laser post guide](../laser-post-guide.md).
