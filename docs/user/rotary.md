# Rotary

A rotary axis turns a cylinder under the cutter. Carvetto lays the job out flat and wraps it onto the cylinder.

1. Enable the rotary axis in the job setup (**Setup > Job Setup...**), giving the cylinder diameter and length.
2. **Setup > Material Setup (rotary)...** describes the stock and the depth reference.
3. **Setup > Wrapped Fluting Layout (rotary)...** and **Setup > Wrapped Spiral Layout (rotary)...** generate
   flutes and spirals around the cylinder.
4. **View > Wrapped View (rotary)** shows the flat job wrapped on the cylinder.
5. Make toolpaths as usual; the post-processor must support the rotary axis.
