- T-0153: V-Carve toolpath (ADR-0025) — `vCarvingToolpath(contours, Tool, optional CuttingData, clearance tools, VCarvingParams)` carves closed regions with a V-bit or ball nose along the medial axis (computed at a third of the chordal tolerance) at the depth whose footprint is the inscribed radius less a margin of chordal/6 (V-bit: radius / tan(half angle)), from a start depth, limited by an optional flat depth and the tool's full-width depth (`CarveDepthLimited`; Z split at the limit's kink). Crossing, touching or self-crossing vectors (and vectors within the polygonisation error of them) are resolved into their even-odd region first (`CrossingVectorsResolved`); the region is split into parts (an outer vector with its holes), each with its own medial axis; skeleton branches joined into few walks, parts ordered with `orderCuts`, every link at safe Z. Flat areas are cleared region by region by `pocketToolpath` with end-mill clearance tools (allowance = the V-bit's footprint at the flat depth) while the V-bit cuts their walls and clears what the end mills cannot reach as Pocket's last rest tool, or by the carving tool itself. A capped medial axis, or one that ignores a boundary, refuses the carve (`MedialAxisCapped`). Accuracy: every point above the limit is carved within the chordal tolerance of its depth (as a footprint radius). Corner sharpening moved to T-0160. `medialAxis` fixes: circumcentres lying exactly on the boundary are refined (an isosceles triangle with an obtuse apex had an empty skeleton; nested squares lacked their corner junctions), contours are classified from up to 16 probes (a one-arc hole near a wall was ignored), and a Gabriel test no longer stops at an exempt sample (a thin band had an empty skeleton). Native record `VCarvingParams` (`"operation": "v_carve"`, one record per tool, format 1.1); `carvetto-cli toolpath v_carve` with `clearance_tools`. Tests: exact cone/wall oracle over a generative sweep including crossing input, pocket-oracle clearance checks, every shipped post against the safety rules, goldens `toolpath/v_carve_grbl.nc` and `cli/sign_v_carve_grbl.nc` (new ctest and CI steps on Linux and Windows); AC2: 5,000 glyph-like regions ≤ 60 s CPU in Release with or without a flat depth (measured ~23–29 s); existing goldens unchanged.
