- T-0153: V-Carve toolpath (ADR-0025) — `vcarveToolpath(contours, Tool, optional CuttingData, clearance tools, VCarveParams)` carves closed regions with a V-bit or ball nose along the medial axis at the depth whose footprint is the inscribed radius less half the chordal tolerance (V-bit: radius / tan(half angle)), from a start depth, limited by an optional flat depth and the tool's full-width depth (`CarveDepthLimited`). Flat areas are cleared by `pocketToolpath` with end-mill clearance tools (Offset or Raster, rest tools, allowance = the V-bit's footprint at the flat depth) while the V-bit cuts their walls, or by the carving tool itself without clearance tools. Regions (vectors with overlapping boxes) get their own medial axis; skeleton branches are joined into few walks, ordered with `orderCuts`; every link at safe Z. A capped medial axis refuses the carve (`MedialAxisCapped`). Corner sharpening deferred (ADR-0025 §6). `medialAxis` now refines circumcentres lying exactly on the boundary (an isosceles triangle with an obtuse apex had an empty skeleton). Native record `VCarveParams` (`"operation": "vcarve"`, one record per tool, format 1.1); `carvetto-cli toolpath vcarve` with `clearance_tools`. Tests: exact cone/wall oracle over a generative sweep (no violation in 21,000 development cases; lateral ≤ 0.87·chordal, coverage ≤ 0.98·chordal), pocket-oracle clearance checks, every shipped post against the safety rules, goldens `toolpath/vcarve_grbl.nc` and `cli/sign_vcarve_grbl.nc` (new ctest and CI steps on Linux and Windows); AC2: 5,000 glyph-like regions ≤ 60 s CPU in Release (measured ~11 s); existing goldens unchanged.
