- T-0183: a vector whose polygon cannot meet the chordal tolerance within the work bounds (an arc past 2^20 chords, ADR-0005 A2) is no longer left out silently: Profile, Pocket, Engrave Fill and V-Carve skip it and report one `ToolpathIssue::ContourRefused` diagnostic per vector (Error in the app) while the other vectors are cut exactly as before (new `geometry::refusedByClippers` / `refusedContourIndices`, ADR-0018 item 5); the DXF and SVG importers warn about such an arc at import; Drill, Thread Mill, Chamfer and Engrave Outline do not polygonise through the clippers and are unaffected.
