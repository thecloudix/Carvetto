- T-0154: no-spindle toolpaths for diamond-drag engraving (owner decision 2026-11-03; ADR-0020, ADR-0024 §6) — Quick Engrave accepts diamond-drag tools again (Outline and Fill, moving exactly like an end mill of the same diameter) and marks their tool snapshot `spindleOff` with spindle speed 0, the only exemption from T-0134's spindle speed > 0 rule; the post leaves out the spindle start lines of HEADER/TOOLCHANGE/NEW_SEGMENT and SPINDLE_ON for such a toolpath and writes the new `SPINDLE_OFF_PAUSE` block before it (every shipped post: `M5`, safe Z, an ASCII message to check that the spindle is stopped or locked, `M0`, safe Z), refuses posts without that block, `[S]` lines that carry other codes and any M3/M4/S word in the drag output, and restarts the spindle afterwards only through TOOLCHANGE + SPINDLE_ON, after the new `SPINDLE_UNLOCK_PAUSE` block (once per drag section, `M0` asking the operator to unlock the spindle; posts without it refuse such jobs); Profile and Pocket refuse diamond-drag tools (`InvalidTool`, pointing to Quick Engrave); job.json `tool.spindle_off` (written only when true, format stays 1.1) is refused on profile, pocket and drill records by the loader, `saveCarv` and the toolpath commands. Every other toolpath kind with spindle speed 0 is still refused by every shipped post (tested); new goldens `post/<post>_drag.nc` for all eight posts; existing goldens unchanged.
