- T-0321: Wrapping level effect, Vector Unwrapper and rotary 3D import (ADR-0114) — a level can be wrapped round the cylinder of a rotary job (`relief::Wrapping`: a flat-modelled relief that runs past the edge of the circumference window folds back in on the other side, highest turn wins; applied after Mirror Mode and before Clipping, made permanent by Bake, undoable `SetReliefLevelWrappingCommand`, viewmodel `setLevelWrapping`); `.carv` format 1.19 stores it (only when a level uses it, every other file keeps its bytes); `geometry::unwrapCrossSection` turns a cross-section into a contour of (circumference, radius) about the contour centre, the workspace centre or a custom point, with optional refinement and an exact inverse, behind `viewmodel::unwrapSelectedVectors`; `relief::meshToRotaryField` maps a mesh onto the cylinder by radial ray casting (axis movement, flip, either axis) and `viewmodel::importMeshIntoRotaryJob` offers Full 3D (with fit to material) and Flat model imports; the app dialogs for these are a follow-up.
