- T-0327: cutter compensation (G41/G42) for Profile toolpaths (ADR-0100) — `ProfileParams::cutterComp` (form check box; `.carv` key `cutter_comp`, format minor 1.15 only when used) makes the toolpath carry the usual tool-centre path with the cut flagged as running under compensation (`Move::comp` Off/Left/Right: climb is left, conventional right; the lead-in engages and the lead-out releases it), so simulation matches the uncompensated result; `cutterCompProgram` derives the programmed path (vector plus allowance: lines moved back by the radius, arcs re-radiused, inside corners joined at the intersection, round corners of allowance 0 omitted) and the post writes it with CUTTER_COMP_LEFT/RIGHT/OFF blocks before the engaging and the releasing move (`[CUTTER_COMP]` = LEFT/RIGHT/OFF; a post lacking a needed block refuses the toolpath). Refused with one `CutterCompRefused` diagnostic and no moves: On, tools but a plain end mill, leads other than a straight lead-in and lead-out at least a tool radius long, zig-zag ramps, curves, arcs the tool fills, neighbouring vectors that merge or are trimmed, leads that do not fit, open vectors in several passes, corners too sharp to compensate. Move caches with compensated moves are version 3 (the laser version 2 layout plus a run list trailer); laser (version 2) and uncompensated (version 1) files are byte-identical to before.
