- T-0210: Properties, Layers, Sheets and History panels. Layers: add, rename, delete (contents moved or deleted), show/lock, colour, reorder, move selection to a layer; Sheets: add, rename, delete and a per-sheet material block; Properties: name, layer, position, size, rotation and per-kind parameters (rectangle, circle, ellipse, polygon, star, text) with unit-aware validated entry, one undo step per edit; an undo History list that jumps to any step. New core commands `RenameObjectCommand` and `SetShapeParamsCommand`, `UndoStack::stepText`; Qt-free `properties_model` and `layers_sheets` in the viewmodel; the self-test drives the panels in the real window.
