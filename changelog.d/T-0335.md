- T-0335: Drill Bank spindle sets (ADR-0111) — every move of a drill bank stroke now names the spindles that drill in it (`Move::bank`, with `Toolpath::bankHead` for the head's offsets, diameters and depths), so preview draws a dot per spindle and the simulator drills one hole per active spindle at its own position and depth (a shallower spindle stops at its own limit); the post writes `SETUP_DRILL_BANK`, `DRILL_BANK_PATTERN`, `DRILL_BANK_EXTEND_DRILL`, `DRILL_BANK_RETRACT_DRILL` and `FINAL_DRILL_BANK_ACTION` with the tags `[DB] [DBX] [DBY] [DL] [CD]` (the first three are required, a post without them refuses the toolpath; documented sample post `posts/samples/drill_bank_linuxcnc_mm.pp` with a golden file); `.carv` format 1.18 and move cache version 4 carry the sets (only when used, hostile caps and record checks, other files byte-identical); the Add menu offers Drill Bank and its form has a per-spindle list editor (active, diameter, depth), driven by the self-test.
