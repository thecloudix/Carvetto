- T-0182: arc polygonisation is bounded — an arc becomes at most 2^20 chords whatever its radius (an arc of radius 1e12 made 70M points in 2.6 s and larger radii hung or ran out of memory; 1e9..1.7e308 now take ≤ 0.04 s), a non-finite arc becomes one point, `polygoniseInto`/`Contour::polygonise` report a coarser-than-tolerance polygon through an optional `coarse` flag, and booleans, offsetting and `OpenPathClipper` refuse such a contour (`BooleanResult::refusedContours`); arcs under the cap are bit-identical to before (ADR-0005 A2); `medialAxis` now ignores (counts in `ignoredContours`, sets `accuracyCapped` when finite) contours with a non-finite coordinate or a coordinate/radius beyond 1e12 mm, so a huge arc no longer hangs or crashes it and a rectangle at an offset of 1e12 is ignored where it used to give a skeleton (ADR-0005 A2).
