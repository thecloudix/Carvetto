# Changelog

## Unreleased

- T-0127: exact tab check for profiles On the vector and with a negative allowance — the conservative On guard is replaced by a raster judged by the sides of the cut path itself (exact nearest point, right across corners; the kerf on each side, the roughing side included; the cut crossed only on the tab's own stretch, so tabs may hold a part together but never borrow another tab's bridge; any remnant the tools leave counts as part; V-bits at their cone radius at the tab top), so On tabs are kept whenever they really hold (short tabs on straight edges, longer tabs on corners) and dropped and reported when any pass severs them; Inside/Outside with an allowance between −r and 0 no longer keeps cut-through tabs silently, and an allowance of exactly −r keeps the Inside direction and part side. Inside/Outside with allowance ≥ 0 and tab-free toolpaths are unchanged (ADR-0016 §4, §14).
- T-0120/T-0124: 2D Profile toolpath (`toolpath/profile.hpp`) — `profileToolpath(contours, Tool, ProfileParams)` returns the toolpath plus diagnostics (e.g. "no room for the tool"); Outside/Inside/On with region (nesting-parity) semantics and Left/Right for open vectors, climb/conventional, allowance, separate last pass (allowance, reverse), start at vector start / optimised / closest on bounding box, tabs (constant number or distance, avoid corners, 3D; footprints, 3D ramps included, are kept clear of the cut start and of the other pass's start, at least a tool radius apart, inside open ends and shorter than the loop, or left out with a per-vector diagnostic, and leads/overcut never run below a tab top over tab material, so every pass reaches its level and no tab is notched), smooth/zig-zag/spiral ramps (distance or angle, on lead-in), straight/circular leads with lead-out and overcut (a gentler variant, a shorter lead or a start moved along the cut when the lead would gouge; dropped only when nothing fits), selection/left-to-right/bottom-to-top/grid/nearest ordering with inner vectors first, V-bit sharp external and internal corners (internal corners only at genuine input corners of exact offsets, the climb checked against the real walls, following curved walls when needed, and honouring the allowance, so the cone never passes a wall), level changes as vertical plunges with every level a full loop, finishing cuts in the roughing order, multi-pass via `PassPlan`; exact arc-preserving offsets, nearby vectors offset together as one region, and any stretch within the tool radius of another vector trimmed. Shared helpers for other operations: `path_walk`, `clearance`, `tab_placement`, `ramp_entry`, `lead_moves`, `cut_order`. With a separate last pass every finishing tab is mapped onto the roughing cut, extended until the roughing tool clears the tab's material (or both are dropped and reported, N of M per vector); tabs whose material any cut would sever (a slot, a lobe, a short tab on a sharp turn of the cut: convex or inside corner, gear root) are dropped and reported with the reason; a tab at a V-bit corner climb is still cut flat; tab thickness is clamped to the cut depth; tabs on profiles On the vector are kept only under a conservative clearance rule and otherwise dropped and reported as cut through (exact rule: T-0127). Hand-checked move-list and Grbl G-code goldens, AC tests and a randomized brute-force gouge test (ADR-0016).
- T-0126: pocket centre-region guard hardening — after pulling in or removing a vertex, every changed edge is checked against the walls with the exact segment distance (and no edge may cross the no-gouge line), falling back to the inset repair; phantoms are told apart by depth (a point of the region at full distance inside the source) instead of a vertex count, so a large region whose small bulge holds most vertices is kept; a region repaired by more than the join tolerance is reported ("trimmed"), and the property test checks every edge on every diameter.
- T-0121/T-0125: pocket toolpath (`toolpath/pocket.hpp`) — `pocketToolpath(contours, tools, PocketParams)` over the even-odd region of closed contours (islands, nested pockets): Offset strategy (inside-out rings, climb/conventional, extra rings where stepover > radius would leave material, complete coverage for every stepover ≤ diameter) or Raster (angle, ≤ stepover pitch, zig-zag), boundary pass First/Last/None for both strategies (owner decision 2026-10-26), pocket allowance, a chordal safety margin so no cut ever comes closer than radius + allowance to a wall, zig-zag ramp plunge along the path (`toolpath/pocket_ramp.hpp`), multi-tool clearance + rest machining (each later tool cuts only where its disc overlaps what the earlier tools left, thin residue included), travel inside the cleared centre region at the previous floor (straight or routed through the offset-ring tree) instead of retracts, so a connected pocket of any shape and any number of islands retracts once (ring bridges); boundary-following links accepted; work caps (pass depth, stepover, chordal tolerance, vertices, and the actual pattern's move count) refuse explosive parameters with a message; inward offsets guarded against Clipper2's inside-out results past the inradius; independent rasterised coverage, island, rest-locality and randomized gouge tests and a hand-checked Grbl golden (ADR-0017). The centre-region guard checks outer and island rings and keeps genuine regions whose vertices overshoot the slack (Clipper's grid, join sag, zero-width spikes) by pulling those vertices in or insetting the region further, and reports any part it still cannot keep; `pocketCentreRegion` exposes the tool-centre region.
- T-0022: distance-exact arc ends and threshold crossings — `closestPoint(ArcSpan)` drops the fixed 1e-9 rad slack (a real distance on large arcs: 4e-5 mm at r = 1.3e5) and returns the arc's end point for any direction outside the sweep, so an end lying beyond an arc's end by more than rounding is an end contact flagged tangent at every radius (1e-3…1e6 mm, double-double reference); two analytic crossings whose chord exceeds the tolerance only by rounding fold into one tangent hit instead of being merged into an untangent one. No result worse than `main` on the T-0007/T-0008/T-0021 families; offsets and booleans unchanged (ADR-0009, T-0022 amendment).
- T-0021: arc/arc crossings without cancellation — crossing circles are solved from the smaller circle (radical-line distance and factored half chord), so arcs of very different radii far from the origin cross where they are (r = 39785 against 0.09: 1e-5 mm error and a false second crossing next to an offset junction gone; error now within a few ulps of the inputs over the sine of the crossing angle); equal radii and pairs with the smaller circle first are bit-identical to before, line/arc already stable and unchanged; ADR-0009 amended, with the regression pair, generative arc/arc and line/arc tests against a double-double reference over radius ratios 1e-6…1e6, and a main-vs-branch brute-force comparison (0 worse).
- T-0008: exact line/arc intersections — an end of either span within tolerance of the other (interior or end) is one hit for every pair kind, tangent unless the end lies on the other span, also inside stretches where a Bezier hugs an arc and next to crossings more than the tolerance away; analytic pairs are solved in a canonical argument order (bit-exact symmetric) and report crossings only where they lie on both spans, so oblique end touches and exact shared end points are no longer missed; near-tangent line/arc and arc/arc crossings further apart than the tolerance are both reported instead of folded into one tangent hit, so a re-crossing next to a contour junction is no longer erased with it; `closestPoint(ArcSpan)` returns parameter 0 for a direction a rounding error before the start (T-0007's wrapper removed); ADR-0009 amended, with regression and generative tests and a main-vs-branch brute-force comparison.
- T-0100: DXF import/export (`carvetto/io/dxf.hpp`) — own group-code reader for ASCII and binary (R12 and R13+) DXF; `importDxf` returns layers (name, ACI/RGB colour, visibility), mm contours tagged by layer and text items (TEXT/ATTRIB/MTEXT with style font, height, alignment, MTEXT formatting stripped) plus merged warnings: LINE, ARC, CIRCLE, ELLIPSE, LWPOLYLINE/POLYLINE bulges as exact arcs, SPLINE (non-rational degree ≤ 3 exact, rational/higher degree and ellipses as Beziers within the chordal tolerance, fit-point-only splines interpolated), nested INSERT/MINSERT with mirror and layer-0 inheritance, OCS, $INSUNITS/$MEASUREMENT units; cycle, depth, instance, span and text budgets; opt-in joining; `exportDxf` writes deterministic R2000 ASCII in mm (LWPOLYLINE bulges with arcs over π split so |bulge| ≤ 1, clamped cubic SPLINEs, CIRCLE, Carvetto XDATA restoring split arcs and contours exactly), out-of-range curves are skipped with warnings before span generation; valid R2000 and R12 fixtures, round-trip (incl. near-full arcs), unit, nested-transform and ASan-run fuzz tests (ADR-0015).
- T-0007: intersection residuals II — a circle or arc touching a convex cubic within tolerance is found at every position (non-converged chord seeds are settled as touch candidates; full circles are walked across their seam; a golden-section early exit that missed narrow dips removed); end touches are reported whichever span's end comes near the other, and a double crossing confined to the last sample piece before a chord or span end is bracketed; a Bezier re-crossing the span it joins within a few tolerances of the junction is found (zero-distance samples no longer defeat the sign test); a cubic's self-intersection is computed analytically, so thin sliver loops are found (loops staying within the tolerance of their double point are cusps); touches near the start of a partial arc no longer get the arc's end parameter, exact tangencies are flagged tangent, and repeated walks into known minima are cut short; ADR-0009 amended, with regression, fine-sweep and generative tests.
- T-0110: original posts for Grbl (inch), Mach3 (mm/inch), Mach4 (mm), LinuxCNC (mm/inch) and Marlin (mm), each written from the controller's public docs, alongside Grbl (mm), which now also lifts to safe Z after the tool-change pause. All posts are golden-tested byte for byte on a shared fixture (square profile, pocket with CW/CCW/full-circle arcs, tool change, peck drill with dwells), and the goldens are checked by an automated safety checklist (units, G90, safe Z before XY rapids, spindle on before cutting, tool change with the spindle off, consistent arc radii, dwell units, spindle off and program end). `posts/README.md` holds the reviewer's checklist and the dialect table. - T-0015: `.carv` zip-layer hardening — members are inflated by Carvetto's own bounded loop over tinfl (consumed input bounded by the declared compressed size, no-progress and over-length detection, CRC/size check), so truncated, corrupted or size-lying streams fail with `InvalidArchive` in microseconds instead of hanging; central-directory entries with overlapping or duplicated local headers are rejected; held bytes after load are capped at max(64 MiB, 32 × archive size); unknown `.json` members are streamed through the strict reader before being kept; a forked per-load-timeout fuzz test (5 000 iterations, 2 seeds) runs in CI; carv timing assertions use CPU time or complexity ratios (ADR-0013 amendment A1).
- T-0005: intersection residuals — `selfIntersections` ignores both junctions of a 2-span closed contour and the closing junction of a 1-span closed Bezier; nearly concentric arcs within tolerance of touching give one tangent hit; Bezier pairs are solved in a canonical argument order (exactly symmetric), on pieces cut where the tangent turns more than 45° (crossings between branches near loops/cusps), with a signed-distance scan for shallow double crossings and touches, touch candidates walked to the local minimum of the distance on the unsplit curves (every closest approach within tolerance is reported once, flagged tangent; hugging curves report crossings and true closest approaches only) and merged hits flagged tangent; single `FillRule` in `geometry/fill_rule.hpp` shared by booleans and point-in-contour; ADR-0009 amended, with regression and generative tests.
