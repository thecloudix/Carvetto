# Changelog

## Unreleased

- T-0021: arc/arc crossings without cancellation — crossing circles are solved from the smaller circle (radical-line distance and factored half chord), so arcs of very different radii far from the origin cross where they are (r = 39785 against 0.09: 1e-5 mm error and a false second crossing next to an offset junction gone; error now within a few ulps of the inputs over the sine of the crossing angle); equal radii and pairs with the smaller circle first are bit-identical to before, line/arc already stable and unchanged; ADR-0009 amended, with the regression pair, generative arc/arc and line/arc tests against a double-double reference over radius ratios 1e-6…1e6, and a main-vs-branch brute-force comparison (0 worse).
- T-0008: exact line/arc intersections — an end of either span within tolerance of the other (interior or end) is one hit for every pair kind, tangent unless the end lies on the other span, also inside stretches where a Bezier hugs an arc and next to crossings more than the tolerance away; analytic pairs are solved in a canonical argument order (bit-exact symmetric) and report crossings only where they lie on both spans, so oblique end touches and exact shared end points are no longer missed; near-tangent line/arc and arc/arc crossings further apart than the tolerance are both reported instead of folded into one tangent hit, so a re-crossing next to a contour junction is no longer erased with it; `closestPoint(ArcSpan)` returns parameter 0 for a direction a rounding error before the start (T-0007's wrapper removed); ADR-0009 amended, with regression and generative tests and a main-vs-branch brute-force comparison.
- T-0100: DXF import/export (`carvetto/io/dxf.hpp`) — own group-code reader for ASCII and binary (R12 and R13+) DXF; `importDxf` returns layers (name, ACI/RGB colour, visibility), mm contours tagged by layer and text items (TEXT/ATTRIB/MTEXT with style font, height, alignment, MTEXT formatting stripped) plus merged warnings: LINE, ARC, CIRCLE, ELLIPSE, LWPOLYLINE/POLYLINE bulges as exact arcs, SPLINE (non-rational degree ≤ 3 exact, rational/higher degree and ellipses as Beziers within the chordal tolerance, fit-point-only splines interpolated), nested INSERT/MINSERT with mirror and layer-0 inheritance, OCS, $INSUNITS/$MEASUREMENT units; cycle, depth, instance, span and text budgets; opt-in joining; `exportDxf` writes deterministic R2000 ASCII in mm (LWPOLYLINE bulges with arcs over π split so |bulge| ≤ 1, clamped cubic SPLINEs, CIRCLE, Carvetto XDATA restoring split arcs and contours exactly), out-of-range curves are skipped with warnings before span generation; valid R2000 and R12 fixtures, round-trip (incl. near-full arcs), unit, nested-transform and ASan-run fuzz tests (ADR-0015).
- T-0007: intersection residuals II — a circle or arc touching a convex cubic within tolerance is found at every position (non-converged chord seeds are settled as touch candidates; full circles are walked across their seam; a golden-section early exit that missed narrow dips removed); end touches are reported whichever span's end comes near the other, and a double crossing confined to the last sample piece before a chord or span end is bracketed; a Bezier re-crossing the span it joins within a few tolerances of the junction is found (zero-distance samples no longer defeat the sign test); a cubic's self-intersection is computed analytically, so thin sliver loops are found (loops staying within the tolerance of their double point are cusps); touches near the start of a partial arc no longer get the arc's end parameter, exact tangencies are flagged tangent, and repeated walks into known minima are cut short; ADR-0009 amended, with regression, fine-sweep and generative tests.
- T-0110: original posts for Grbl (inch), Mach3 (mm/inch), Mach4 (mm), LinuxCNC (mm/inch) and Marlin (mm), each written from the controller's public docs, alongside Grbl (mm), which now also lifts to safe Z after the tool-change pause. All posts are golden-tested byte for byte on a shared fixture (square profile, pocket with CW/CCW/full-circle arcs, tool change, peck drill with dwells), and the goldens are checked by an automated safety checklist (units, G90, safe Z before XY rapids, spindle on before cutting, tool change with the spindle off, consistent arc radii, dwell units, spindle off and program end). `posts/README.md` holds the reviewer's checklist and the dialect table. Adds a starter tool database (`data/tools/starter_tools.json`, 29 tools, conservative softwood, hardwood/MDF, acrylic and aluminium data for a Generic Router) with load, validate, byte-identical export and chip-load sanity tests. Firmware and controller caveats are documented: Marlin's `M0` needs an LCD or `EMERGENCY_PARSER`, and Mach tool changes must not be set to "ignore". `tools/regen_golden.py` regenerates the post goldens and the canonical starter database through hidden `[.regen]` tests; the output must be hand-reviewed before it is committed.
- T-0006: open-offset quality for toolpaths — `offsetOpenContour` is ~25–40× faster at a large distance relative to the feature size (2000-step random walk at d = 2: ~1.2 s → < 0.05 s Release) via witness-cached early-exit pruning, up-front discarding of raw elements within d of one input line and outer-arc-only vertex circles; Sharp/Square joins at arc corners extend arcs into their remaining gap (true mitres, e.g. keyhole d = 0.25 → mitre at 0.304), capped at 90° and mitreLimit·d from the vertex; chain junctions are snapped so consecutive spans share end points exactly with no backward steps (overlapping pieces on one line or circle, e.g. the collinear chamfers/mitres of steep zig-zags, are merged along that carrier), and the T-0004 checks and timing test follow (exact continuity, CPU time) (ADR-0012 amendment A1).
- T-0015: `.carv` zip-layer hardening — members are inflated by Carvetto's own bounded loop over tinfl (consumed input bounded by the declared compressed size, no-progress and over-length detection, CRC/size check), so truncated, corrupted or size-lying streams fail with `InvalidArchive` in microseconds instead of hanging; central-directory entries with overlapping or duplicated local headers are rejected; held bytes after load are capped at max(64 MiB, 32 × archive size); unknown `.json` members are streamed through the strict reader before being kept; a forked per-load-timeout fuzz test (5 000 iterations, 2 seeds) runs in CI; carv timing assertions use CPU time or complexity ratios (ADR-0013 amendment A1).
- T-0005: intersection residuals — `selfIntersections` ignores both junctions of a 2-span closed contour and the closing junction of a 1-span closed Bezier; nearly concentric arcs within tolerance of touching give one tangent hit; Bezier pairs are solved in a canonical argument order (exactly symmetric), on pieces cut where the tangent turns more than 45° (crossings between branches near loops/cusps), with a signed-distance scan for shallow double crossings and touches, touch candidates walked to the local minimum of the distance on the unsplit curves (every closest approach within tolerance is reported once, flagged tangent; hugging curves report crossings and true closest approaches only) and merged hits flagged tangent; single `FillRule` in `geometry/fill_rule.hpp` shared by booleans and point-in-contour; ADR-0009 amended, with regression and generative tests.
- T-0012: native `.carv` file I/O — `saveCarv`/`loadCarv` (bytes or path) write/read the zip container (miniz 3.0.2, pinned by SHA256) with `manifest.json` (format 1.0, app version) and canonical `job.json` (job, sheets, layers, objects with exact shortest-repr doubles, creation params, groups, variables, active layer/sheet, id high-water mark); deterministic archives (sorted keys, fixed member order, zeroed timestamps) so save → load → save is byte-identical; unknown fields, parameter kinds and members preserved via `CarvExtras` as canonical text; untrusted JSON is streamed through a strict pull parser straight into records (no DOM) under one archive-wide resource budget, with archive size/ratio caps, a linear group-structure pre-check and ids below 2^62, so decompression and structure bombs fail fast with `ResourceLimit`; clear `CarvError`s for newer major versions, damage, malformed JSON, bad references and integrity failures, with fuzz and bomb tests (ADR-0013).
- T-0014: document hardening — `checkIntegrity()` reports `IdKeyMismatch` for an object whose stored id differs from its map key and counts stored ids in `DuplicateId`, and runs in O(N log N) on deep or wide group trees (iterative loop detection, set-based child lookup; tests with a 60 000-deep chain, a 200 000-child group and a loop closed at depth 50 000) (ADR-0007 amendment A3); the 10 000-op undo/redo timing test now measures process CPU time (best of 3) and asserts time(20k) < 3 × time(10k) to catch O(n²) regressions under parallel load; CI runs `ctest -j8 --repeat until-fail:3`.
- T-0004: `offsetOpenContour` returns every chain (`std::vector<Contour>`, ordered along the input) instead of the longest; chains never cross or touch themselves (cut into tail / closed loop / tail), mitre and chamfer stubs past a trim point are pruned, overlapping mitre extensions deduplicated, and grid-accelerated splitting/pruning offsets a 2000-span polyline in well under 0.1 s (Release) (ADR-0012).
- T-0013: preset-id guard — `AddObjectCommand`, `AddLayerCommand` and `AddSheetCommand` refuse (no-op, nothing recorded) a caller-supplied id already used by any sheet, layer or object or below the generator's next id, so an id freed by undo cannot be re-taken; `checkIntegrity()` reports `DuplicateId` for an id shared across kinds; regression and fixed-seed randomized undo/redo tests (ADR-0007 amendment A2).
- T-0011: id overflow guard — `IdGenerator::kMaxId` (2^62) ceiling in `document/ids.hpp`; the generator never wraps (`tryAllocate`, `canAllocate`, `remaining`, `exhausted`; `reserveThrough` clamps), allocating commands (add object/layer/sheet, paste, group) become no-ops when ids run out, `checkIntegrity()` reports `IdOutOfRange` / `IdNotBelowNext`, with unit tests and a fixed-seed near-ceiling randomized test (ADR-0007 amendment A1).
- T-0060: Qt app shell — main window with 2D/3D view tabs, rulers, sheet/layer selectors, left (Design, Sheets, Layers, Components, Library) and right (Toolpaths) docks, status-bar cursor position in display units, File > New Job dialog, and a headless `carvetto --self-test`; view logic (view transform, nice-number rulers with exact labels, screen-tolerance polygonisation, cursor formatting, New Job validation) lives in the Qt-free `carvetto_viewmodel` library with Catch2 tests (ADR-0014).
- T-0002: span intersections and contour utilities — `geometry/intersect.hpp` (analytic line/arc intersections with `tangent` flag and collinear/coincident `Overlap` intervals, Bezier pairs by polygonisation + Newton/golden-section refinement, contour–contour and self-intersection detection) and `geometry/contour_ops.hpp` (exact signed area/orientation, winding number and point-in-contour with even-odd/non-zero fill rules, closest point on span/contour), with Catch2 tests (ADR-0009).
- T-0003: polygon offsets and booleans via Clipper2 1.4.0 (FetchContent, pinned) — `offsetContours` (round / sharp-with-mitre-limit / square joins, open contours outlined), `offsetOpenContour` (exact single-sided left/right offset of lines and arcs: joins at outside corners, arcs extended for sharp/square corners, consumed spans pruned so every point stays ≥ d from the input), `booleanOp` (union / difference / intersection / xor, NonZero or EvenOdd fill, open subjects clipped) and `unionContours`; fixed-point scale `kFixedPointScale` documented in ADR-0006; Catch2 tests.
- T-0030: `.pp` post-processor interpreter — parser for global settings, `VAR` specs (A/C modes, incremental, prefix, `[+]W.D` format, multiplier), `begin/end` blocks with quoted output lines and `+` comments; interpreter for header/tool change/new segment/move/footer blocks with first-move and missing-block fallbacks, modal suppression, line numbering with wrap, inch scaling, arc I/J and R forms with linearisation, inverse time and rotary wrap; shipped `posts/grbl_mm.pp` with a golden-file test (ADR-0011).
- T-0010: document model — `Job` (single/double/rotary, mm internally with display `Units`, Z-zero, XY datum + offset), `Sheet`, `Layer`, `Object` (vector with contours + creation-parameter variant, bitmap placeholder, group), monotonic 64-bit ids, `Selection`, `DocumentVariables` (naming rule, references, text import/export), command-based `UndoStack` with coalescing and macros, the concrete object/layer/sheet/job/variable commands with group-integrity rules (ungroup splices, delete-layer dissolves groups, layer/sheet moves follow the group tree), and `Document::checkIntegrity()` exercised by a randomized undo/redo test, with Catch2 tests (ADR-0007).
- T-0040: toolpath core — `Move` (rapid/feed/plunge/retract/arcCW/arcCCW/dwell, 3D endpoint, arc centre, feed override, dwell seconds), `Vec3`, `Toolpath` with `ToolSnapshot`, bounds and length by kind, `PassPlan` (exact-step / equalised passes, last-pass thickness), contour linking with safe-Z rapids and plunge/retract, and a deterministic trapezoidal-acceleration time estimate, with Catch2 tests (ADR-0008).
