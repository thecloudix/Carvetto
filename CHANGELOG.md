# Changelog

## Unreleased

- T-0151: Quick Engrave toolpath (ADR-0024) — `engraveToolpath(contours, Tool, optional CuttingData, EngraveParams)` marks vectors at a depth in a number of equal passes (`engraveLevels`), with a V-bit, engraving tool, end mill or ball nose (others refused; diamond-drag tools refused until spindle-off support exists, owner decision 2026-11-03). Outline: the tool centre follows every vector, closed ones round once per level stepping down at the start, open ones back and forth, arcs kept and Beziers flattened within the chordal tolerance, ordered nearest first through the shared `orderCuts`; empty, zero-length and gapped vectors skipped and reported. Fill: the closed vectors cleared by `pocketToolpath` itself (Offset or Raster pattern, raster angle, boundary pass last, pass depth = depth / passes; its diagnostics worded for the fill), so the tool's width at the engrave depth (a V-bit's cone there) stays inside the vectors; stepover in mm (0 = the cutting data's) at most that width; open vectors ignored and reported. Native record: `EngraveParams` in `ToolpathParams` (`"operation": "engrave"`, snake_case params like params.json, format stays 1.1); `carvetto-cli toolpath engrave <params.json> <in.carv> <out.carv>` with every key in `help toolpath`. Tested against the pocket oracle (generative, V-bits/end mills/ball noses) and through every shipped post against the safety rules; new goldens `toolpath/engrave_grbl.nc` and `cli/part_engrave_grbl.nc` (third CLI end-to-end ctest and CI step on Linux and Windows); existing goldens unchanged.
- T-0150: drilling toolpath (ADR-0022) — `drillToolpath(contours, Tool, optional CuttingData, DrillParams)` drills one hole at the area centroid of every closed vector (exact for lines and arcs, Beziers by exact quadrature; symmetric shapes get their exact centre), with a drill or an end mill (other tools refused), start and cut depth, optional peck drilling in passes of the cutting data's pass depth (retract to the cutting start + retract gap, or the gap above the depth just reached), an optional dwell at the bottom of every pass, and hole order through the shared `orderCuts` (selection, left-to-right, bottom-to-top, grid, nearest); every rapid at safe Z, plunges at the plunge rate, no canned cycles. Open, empty, gapped and zero-area vectors and vectors whose centroid lies outside them are skipped and reported, holes wider than their vector drilled and reported, coincident holes drilled once (new `ToolpathIssue`s appended). Native record: `DrillParams` in `ToolpathParams` (`"operation": "drill"`, snake_case params like params.json, format stays 1.1); `carvetto-cli toolpath drill <params.json> <in.carv> <out.carv>` with every key in `help toolpath`. Tested through every shipped post against the safety rules; new goldens `toolpath/drill_holes_grbl.nc` and `cli/part_drill_grbl.nc` (second CLI end-to-end ctest and CI step on Linux and Windows); existing goldens unchanged.
- T-0138: `offsetContours` drops grid-level dust — result rings into which no disk of radius 4e-6 mm fits (four Clipper2 grid units, more only beyond ~2.8e8 mm; judged by Clipper2's erosion, inexact on acute rings: removed inradius up to ~5.6e-6 mm, kept down to ~3.6e-6 mm) are removed, with any ring nested inside them, so insets within a few nanometres of collapse no longer return 3-vertex slivers (|area| down to 1e-11 mm², some wound against their nesting or nested in a same-wound genuine ring) and the contract "a result contour wound against the outermost input is a genuine hole" holds as written (ADR-0006 §8). Rings narrow only by mean width (a genuine region with a hair-thin tail) are kept by an erosion test; the threshold is independent of the tolerance. Generative orientation-versus-nesting test near collapse; Pocket/Profile output and goldens unchanged.

- T-0136: native toolpath storage residuals — `saveCarv` verifies its output with the loader's caches in validate-only mode (same checks and messages, no second decoded copy; save peak 724 → 218 MiB for 8 M raster moves, 184 → 60 MiB for 2 M, Release), clearer limit messages (how many computed toolpaths fit next to preserved members, and a computed toolpath past position 9,999 named with its position), over-long names reported as "name too long" with their job.json field instead of a decompression-bomb ratio error, the interim counting pass shares the load budget (no reservation the budget then refuses), and non-finite toolpath parameters (`"NaN"`, `"Infinity"`) refused by the loader, `saveCarv` and `Add/SetToolpathCommand` (`nonFiniteParam`; ADR-0021). - T-0018: inward offsets past the inradius vanish instead of coming back inside out — `offsetContours` no longer turns an over-shrunk region into its point reflection (a 17 mm circle inset by 19.2 mm returned a 2.2 mm circle, by 30 mm a 13 mm one; every polygonised curve, all join types, holes included). Root cause in Clipper2 1.4.0 (`ClipperOffset` skipped the original vertex at concave joins flatter than ~8°); the upstream fix (#873/#916) is backported by a one-line, idempotent configure-time patch (ADR-0006 §6). Shapes with several lobes keep exactly their surviving lobes; holes grow and stay holes. Generative test against a brute-force inradius/distance oracle (circles, polygons, needle stars, arcs, Beziers, dumbbells, with holes, both orientations, all joins). Pocket's `insetBy` phantom guard is kept as defence (ADR-0017 §4; it no longer fires); Profile no longer reports `TrimmedNearVector` for merged-region phantoms. Valid insets are the same regions, but vertices can shift along near-straight edges (≤ 2.4 µm Hausdorff on raw offsets), so many Pocket toolpaths change slightly (vertices sliding up to ~0.04 mm along the path, ≤ ~3 µm off it, occasional ±1 retract on rest machining) and Profile gains correct "no room for the tool" diagnostics where phantom offsets used to appear; all safety checks unchanged, goldens unchanged. Clipper2's handling of open paths lying exactly on a clip boundary (returned by the intersection, the difference or both, by sweep position) is documented in ADR-0006 §7 and pinned by a test.

Goldens unchanged.
- T-0129: Profile refuses an allowance below minus the tool radius (owner decision 2026-11-01): `profileToolpath` returns no moves and one `InvalidParameters` diagnostic naming the allowance and the limit ("allowance -4 mm is below minus the tool radius (-3 mm at the cut depth) …"; r is half the cutting width at start + cut depth, so a V-bit's cone radius there) when the allowance, or with a separate last pass the allowance + last-pass allowance, is below −r; a separate last pass whose finishing pass alone crosses the vector is refused as a whole (no roughing on its own). - T-0127: exact tab check for profiles On the vector and with a negative allowance — the conservative On guard is replaced by judging each tab by the pieces of material the cut leaves at the tab layer (tools as wide as there, a V-bit's cone at the tab top): a tab is kept when its own material joins every piece near it (exactly and with the tools widened by 0.055 mm, so necks are about 0.1 mm or more), dropped and reported when it would hold nothing or is cut through; other tabs count as cut, so a tab never borrows another's bridge. Short On tabs on straight edges and longer tabs on corners are kept; Inside/Outside with an allowance between −r and 0 no longer keeps cut-through tabs, and an allowance of exactly −r keeps the Inside direction and part side. Pieces of material inside a cut that no kept tab holds are reported on a best-effort basis (known gaps tracked in T-0135). Inside/Outside with allowance ≥ 0 and tab-free toolpaths are unchanged (ADR-0016 §4, §14).

- T-0006/T-0017: open-offset quality for toolpaths — `offsetOpenContour` is ~25–40× faster at a large distance relative to the feature size (2000-step random walk at d = 2: ~1.2 s → < 0.05 s Release) via witness-cached early-exit pruning, up-front discarding of raw elements within d of one input line and outer-arc-only vertex circles; - T-0022: distance-exact arc ends and threshold crossings — `closestPoint(ArcSpan)` drops the fixed 1e-9 rad slack (a real distance on large arcs: 4e-5 mm at r = 1.3e5) and returns the arc's end point for any direction outside the sweep, so an end lying beyond an arc's end by more than rounding is an end contact flagged tangent at every radius (1e-3…1e6 mm, double-double reference); two analytic crossings whose chord exceeds the tolerance only by rounding fold into one tangent hit instead of being merged into an untangent one.
